GRANAMA

Unscramble into a famous name

    How to play

    The clue is an anagram of a famous person's name. Rearrange all its letters to find them in 6 guesses. The empty tiles show how long each word of the name is.

    Type, or tap the clue's letters. Each guess must use every letter exactly once.

    S

    Green: right letter in the right place. A word turns fully green and gets a green border once it's right, and it stays locked in for your next guesses.

    Stuck? Hints reveal the category, another anagram, a nudge, then the first letters.

    A new daily puzzle every day; Practice mode is unlimited.
